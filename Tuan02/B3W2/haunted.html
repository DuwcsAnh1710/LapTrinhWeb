The Haunted Maze

When the sun goes down, our famous Corn Maze takes on a spooky new life! Join us for the scariest Halloween attraction in Council Bluffs. Our Haunted Maze features professional actors, amazing special effects, and spine-tingling surprises around every corner.

This is not your ordinary haunted house - it's a fully immersive experience set in our massive corn maze. With over 4 miles of terrifying trails, you never know what - or who - might be lurking in the darkness.

Hours

  • Every Friday and Saturday in October
  • 7:00 PM - Midnight
  • Special Halloween Night Event: October 31st

Admission

  • General Admission: $12
  • Fast Pass (skip the line): $20
  • Group Rate (10 or more): $10 each

Important Information

  • Not recommended for children under 12
  • No refunds due to weather or fear
  • Proper footwear required
  • No touching the actors, they won't touch you
