Petting Barn

Visit our family-friendly Petting Barn, where children of all ages can get up close and personal with our friendly farm animals! We have over 100 gentle animals waiting to meet you, including:

  • Baby goats
  • Lambs
  • Calves
  • Rabbits
  • Chickens
  • Miniature horses

Activities

  • Bottle feeding baby animals (scheduled times)
  • Grooming sessions
  • Educational presentations
  • Farm life demonstrations

Hours

  • Monday - Friday: 9 am - 4 pm
  • Saturday & Sunday: 11 am - 3 pm

Admission

  • Adults: $8
  • Children (3-12): $6
  • Under 3: Free
  • School Groups: Special rates available

Note: For the safety of our animals and guests, all visitors must use our hand-washing stations before and after visiting the animals. Food and drinks are not allowed in the barn area.
